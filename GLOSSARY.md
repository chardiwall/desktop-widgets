# Desktop Widgets

The settings app manages three widgets that appear on the user's desktop.

## Language

**Ayah reminder**
The widget that displays a daily Quran verse in Arabic with its English translation.

**Clock**
The widget that displays the time and date.

**Avatar**
The animated desktop character. The character can appear on the clock or at its own desktop position. It can stand on the clock when both widgets are on the same display.

**Card**
The visible container for the ayah reminder or clock.

**Card style**
A preset for a card's appearance, such as glass, solid, or minimal.

**Accent colour**
The highlight colour chosen independently for each card.

**Outfit**
A selectable appearance for the avatar's clothing.

**Widget theme**
A coordinated set of colours and appearance choices for one widget. A theme can be a preset or a custom look.

**Placement**
The display and screen position assigned to a widget.

**Standalone placement**:
The Avatar's saved display and screen position when it is away from the Clock.
