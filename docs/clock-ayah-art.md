# Clock and Ayah artwork

This first round used the built-in ImageGen tool and the five reference images. Each atlas uses its left column for Clock and its right column for Ayah. The first row is variant A and the second is variant B. The original 720 × 320 PNG crops kept their transparency and tapered the inner edge. The [Clock atmosphere artwork](clock-atmosphere-art.md) documents the current Clock scenes.

## graphite

Reference attachment: `codex-clipboard-8PnCbA.png`.

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: graphite transparent desktop widget illustration atlas. The attached image is a mood and craft reference only. Produce a single landscape atlas with FOUR individual WIDE scenery pieces, in a precise equal 2x2 grid. Read order upper left, upper right, lower left, lower right. Each quadrant is a standalone scene filling that quadrant's RIGHT half, with painterly shadows dispersing into real transparency by the center and left. No gutters needed, but leave each piece cleanly contained within its own quadrant. Let it meet the right and bottom edges with intentional cropping. Art direction: charcoal, lilac, faint silver, occasional warm gold; elegant Islamic illuminated manuscript illustration, contextually accurate architecture and geometric ornament, spectral atmospherics, pleasing to study at 460 px wide. Scenes: upper left: a path of silver dawn mist following the curve of a valley, with violet rock flowers; upper right: a close sheltered bank of wet violets beside the origin of that mist; lower left: a high snow-edged mountain rising above purple cloud; lower right: a low protected mountain spring and two dark ridgelines leading to that same mountain. True transparent background visible in the left 35% of each quadrant. Avoid letters of any script, text, headings, numerals, decorative frames, mockup, UI, and four solid rectangular backdrops. Each scene is a living soft-edged vignette, with fine focal detail and muted values so foreground text and a small cream figure will read clearly.
