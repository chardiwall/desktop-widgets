# Restrained Clock and Ayah artwork

This earlier artwork pass used sprite sheets from the built-in ImageGen tool. The supplied Lantern A screenshot was the reference for scale and contrast. Some of its Clock ornaments are still used alongside the [Clock atmosphere artwork](clock-atmosphere-art.md). The Blueprint B Ayah layers are in `desktop_widgets/widgets/ayah/art/`.

## lantern

> Use case: illustration-story. Production asset: four transparent decorative stamps for the lantern desktop clock. The supplied Lantern A screenshot is a reference for the quiet scale and restraint of the artwork only. Design an exact two-by-two sprite sheet on real transparent alpha. Keep a clean gap down the middle and across the middle. TOP LEFT: small pierced lantern hanging from a single olive sprig. TOP RIGHT: narrow gold crescent and three blue and gold floral tezhip curls. Each top asset is a small isolated corner ornament, drawn as sparse fine lines and velvety aged detail, no backdrop, floating centered in its cell. BOTTOM LEFT: distant minaret silhouettes and thin terrace at dusk. BOTTOM RIGHT: low line of garden cypresses behind a stone balustrade. Each bottom asset is a short, exceptionally thin horizontal silhouette strip only, centered in its cell, no taller than one fifth of the cell and with lots of genuine transparent air above it. No sky, no full picture, no rectangular patches, no thick solid fill, no white glow or white fringe, no gradients that look like a photo, no framing or dividing lines, no text or lettering. Art direction: antique brass, midnight mauve, indigo; delicate Islamic manuscript marginalia and low-relief ink washes, faded colors, understated like a detail at the edge of a page. The shapes are meant to be faint and small on a real desktop card, with typography entirely separate. Preserve recognizable silhouettes, sparse highlights, and a genuine transparent background around every motif.

## blueprint-ayah

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: two sparse transparent border details for a Qur'an verse card named Blueprint B, a quiet astronomical theme in indigo. Treat the attached Lantern A image as a reference for restraint only. Make a precise left/right two-cell sprite sheet, true transparent alpha, with clear space between objects and around them. LEFT CELL: small piece of Islamic geometrical star tiling drawn as hairline patinated silver-blue lines, an isolated corner design with generous air between repeating shapes. RIGHT CELL: a very wide, extremely low strip of faint slate-blue pencil constellation lines and an impression of a roofline from an observatory terrace, with tapering ends. The roofline and the star links are low and faint. No photographic details, no solid background, no glowing white, no labels, no text, no rectangular color patches. A sober manuscript marginalia treatment, the details are meant to be almost watermarked.
